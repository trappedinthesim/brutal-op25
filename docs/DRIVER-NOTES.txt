RTL-SDR Blog V4 USB recovery

Upstream source remains pinned to rtlsdrblog/rtl-sdr-blog commit
aed0ea19f3a273370a13c9009b96313c75d54c7b.

build/rtl-usb-retry.patch adapts the demod-write recovery described in:
https://github.com/steve-m/librtlsdr/pull/58
https://www.mail-archive.com/osmocom-sdr@lists.osmocom.org/msg01344.html

Only LIBUSB_ERROR_PIPE is retried, once, after the normal demod read.
Successful writes are not skipped or cached. Other errors and a failed
retry retain the original failure reporting and return-value checks.
Docker verifies the patch against the pinned source before compilation.

Local hardware validation: RTL-SDR Blog V4 through usbipd/WSL/Docker,
AARRS Northeast Simulcast. Bounded tests check control-channel acquisition
and control/voice retuning; they do not establish audible voice quality,
all host-controller compatibility, or SDRplay hardware operation.

The image build context remains an explicit source-file allowlist.
Credentials, captured listings, saved profiles, and diagnostic scripts
are not included in the image.
